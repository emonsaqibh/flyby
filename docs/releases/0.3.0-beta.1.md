The first beta of the rebuilt Flyby.

### Install

```sh
curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/emonsaqibh/flyby/main/install.sh | bash -s -- --beta
```

macOS 14 or later, Apple silicon or Intel. Already running 0.1 or 0.2? The same command replaces it and keeps your settings.

### What's new

- **Google AI Mode in your own Google session.** Connect Safari or Firefox in Settings › Google Account and AI Mode runs signed in as you — which is what keeps Google's "are you human?" checks away. Only `google.*` cookies are copied, into Flyby's own private store.
- **Answers in Flyby's own UI.** AI Mode's answer — headings, lists, tables, cited sources — is read from Google's page as it streams and drawn natively, the same way as Gemini's. If Google does ask for a CAPTCHA or consent, the page appears in place with a banner; deal with it once and Flyby carries on.
- **Liquid Glass.** A Spotlight-style glass pill whose ↩ and provider controls morph out as you type, a glass answer panel, and a new sidebar Settings window. macOS 27's glass refinements apply automatically; macOS 14–15 get the classic look.
- **Stop, retry, copy:** ⌘. / ⌘R / ⌘⇧C.
- **Gemini** now defaults to `gemini-3.8-flash` (2.5 models shut down on 16 October), with readable errors and a retry on rate limits.
- **Update checks.** Flyby tells you when there's a newer version (Settings › General › Updates; turn on "Include beta versions" to hear about betas).
- Lots of fixes: the shortcut can no longer get stuck off after recording, the installer no longer asks to move itself on every launch, typing "@" on German layouts no longer triggers a double-tap ⌥, and more.

### Known limitations

- **This beta hasn't been tested on real hardware yet** — it was built and unit-tested in CI. Connecting your Google account and reading AI Mode answers are the parts most likely to need fixes; please report what you see.
- Not notarized: install with the command above rather than downloading the zip in a browser.
- Chrome-family browsers can't be connected (Chrome ties its Google session to the Mac); use Safari or Firefox, or sign in within Flyby.
